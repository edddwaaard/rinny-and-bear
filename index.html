Meet the Maker Behind Rinny & Bear

Hi, I’m Amy, the hands and heart behind Rinny & Bear.

Each charm is handmade in Buckinghamshire, UK, with care and attention to detail, aimed at the joy of gifting something meaningful.

A Little Care Goes a Long Way

Please read these simple care and safety notes to help keep your charm beautiful and safe.

  • Not a toy: Bag charms are decorative accessories and not intended for unsupervised play.
  • Small parts: Contains beads and clasps that may present a choking hazard.
  • Care: Wipe clean with a damp cloth. Avoid water submersion or excessive pulling.
  • Decorative use only: Handle gently to preserve the handmade finish.
