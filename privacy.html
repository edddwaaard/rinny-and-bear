How this website uses information.

Information we collect

This website does not ask you to create an account or submit personal information. If you contact Rinny & Bear through Instagram, that conversation is handled by Instagram under its own privacy policy.

Optional Google Analytics

With your permission, this website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. It may collect information such as page views, approximate location, device type, and how visitors reached the site. Analytics is not loaded unless you select “Accept analytics” in the consent notice.

Your choice

You can reject analytics when the consent notice appears. Your choice is stored in your browser so the notice does not appear on every visit. To change your choice, clear this site's stored data in your browser and revisit the site.

This site uses Google Fonts, Google Analytics when accepted, Instagram links, and GitHub Pages hosting. These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.
